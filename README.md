**Latest beta: 0.9.3** · Online server: **https://rebirth-game.duckdns.org**

Open **https://rebirth-game.duckdns.org** on any phone, PC, Mac, iPhone (Safari), Xbox (Edge) or tablet.

## Play with friends on any platform
Open **MENU** to find your **friend code** (like `ABCD-1234`). Add friends by their code to see who's online and
on what platform, invite them, or join their game. Phones, PC, Steam, Epic and console browsers can all
play together.

## Controls
- **Phone:** joystick and on-screen buttons.
- **PC:** WASD to move, mouse to aim (click the game to capture the mouse), left click to fire, wheel or Q to switch guns, E to interact.
- **Controller:** Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo layouts, each with its own button names.

## What's new in 0.9.3
- Android: on first launch the app asks for access to a folder for mods. Copy mod folders into it and they show up under Settings > Mods.

## What's new in 0.9.2
- Every skill works now, and each one says what it does at every level
- Stealth: zombies have to hear or see you. The panel by the map shows your noise and whether you're Hidden, Heard or Spotted
- Sprint (push the joystick all the way out) and crouch
- Aim down the sights, reload animations and a RELOAD button
- Phones: a menu wheel and one INTERACT button instead of a screen full of buttons
- Bites can infect you, gear wears out and gets repaired, crafting, explosive barrels
- PC and Windows downloads are still 0.8.0 and update themselves to the newest game when online

## What's new in 0.8.0
- **Automatic updates:** the Android and PC apps (and the iPhone app once it is on TestFlight) now download new versions of the game by themselves, the same as the browser version. Install 0.8.0 once and you won't need to download new betas for game updates.
- Realistic people and zombies, and each zombie shows how it was turned (bites, mauling, long dead)
- Gore: limbs come off, heads pop, blood and gibs (set it to Off, Reduced or Full in Settings)
- Melee weapons at the trader: knife, bat, crowbar, machete, axe and sledgehammer
- Mod support: all game content is in sorted files, and mods can add weapons, zombies, items, props and buildings. See MODDING.md in the game files, and Settings > Mods to turn mods on or off. On PC, put mods in the `mods` folder next to the game.
- Fixed: picking up ammo while holding a melee weapon
