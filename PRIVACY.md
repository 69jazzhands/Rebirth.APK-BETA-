# Rebirth: privacy policy

*Last updated: November 27, 2026*

Rebirth is a zombie survival game. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what control
you have. In short: we collect only what online play needs, we show no ads, and we never sell anything.

## What we collect
**When you play online** (friends, the shared city, private games), the Rebirth server stores:
- **A random device ID** made by the game on first launch. It isn't your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID; it only signs you back in.
- **Your player name**, the one you type in or your Steam or Epic display name.
- **Your friend code** and your **friends list** (who you added, who added you).
- **Your online status** while you play: online or offline, the platform you're on, and which game room you're in. Only your friends can see it.
- **Steam or Epic account ID**, only if you play the PC version through Steam or the Epic Games Store. It's used to sign you in and to find Steam friends who also play.

**During a match**, your character's position and actions are sent to the other players in the same game
so you can play together. This information isn't kept after the match.

**On your device only**, the game saves your settings (graphics, controls), your sign-in token and your
progress. This never leaves your device.

## What we don't collect
- No location (GPS), contacts, photos, microphone or camera
- No advertising IDs, no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking
- No payment information. Rebirth has no in-app purchases right now.

## How it's used
Only to run the game: signing you in, showing your friends list, sending invites and letting players see
each other in the same game. We never sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## Where it's stored
On Rebirth's own game server, hosted by Oracle Cloud in the United States. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

## Your choices
- **Play offline.** Solo play doesn't send anything to the server.
- **Remove friends** at any time from MENU > Friends.
- **Delete your account.** Email the address below with your friend code (shown in MENU > Friends), and your account, name and friends list will be deleted within 30 days.

## Children
Rebirth contains zombie violence and weapons and isn't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly
collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us
and we'll delete it.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
